# Taute Group owl artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool, not a paid API integration. The project asset is `public/assets/taute-owl.png` (420 × 420, transparent PNG). The original illustration is preserved separately from the public website.

Used in the homepage starting-point guide, linking visitors to Cyber Risk Discovery, and as a quiet companion beside invitations on Services, Approach and About, with a welcoming placement on Contact. The folded roadmap reinforces its guiding role. Empty alternative text avoids repeating the adjacent link text for screen readers; explicit image dimensions reserve its layout space.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: small custom illustrated mascot for Taute Group, a professional business-first cybersecurity advisory website. Create a single friendly, thoughtful owl character, gently cartoonish editorial illustration with refined hand-drawn navy outlines, warm ivory feathers, a small muted brick-red scarf, subtle terracotta details. Owl stands next to a small open folded roadmap with simple lines and a red route dot, one wing lightly resting on the map. Calm attentive expression, charming but mature, no childish giant eyes, no aggressive posture. Clean flat colours with very subtle paper-like texture, elegant simple silhouette readable at 150px. Full character visible, centered, square composition, generous empty transparent space, genuinely transparent background. Palette navy #152b36, warm ivory #f8f7f2, muted red #a33f3c. No green, text, letters, logos, gradients, padlocks, shields, hacker imagery, photorealism, background scenery or extra characters.
