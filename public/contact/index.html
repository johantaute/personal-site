Starting is simple

What happens next?

  1. Introductory conversation

    We listen to your concerns and learn what matters to your business.

  2. Agree your scope

    You receive a proposal with the work, deliverables and fees to agree before we begin.

  3. Discovery and assessment

    For Cyber Risk Discovery, we speak with your stakeholders and review agreed evidence.

  4. Management readout

    We walk through your findings, priorities and roadmap together, with time for questions.

  5. Optional ongoing support

    You choose whether regular guidance or cybersecurity leadership would help you move forward.

This is the typical Discovery journey. Other services follow the scope we agree with you.
