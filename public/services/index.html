A clear starting point

Cyber Risk Discovery

A facilitated working session — structured like a game, grounded in real incidents — followed by rigorous analysis. You get clarity without a technical audit.

Best suited to

You want to understand your risk without sitting through a checkbox audit — and your team would rather argue it out in a room than fill in a spreadsheet.

What the assessment includes

In the session, we map your critical operations, systems, data and supplier dependencies, then work through realistic cyber threat scenarios one by one — drawn as scenario cards, argued out loud by the people who run the business. Each scenario is tested against what protection is proven, what is merely assumed, and what no one has checked. After the session, we score and prioritise everything into your risk register and 90-day roadmap.

What you receive

  • Prioritised risk register
    Your key risks, why they matter to your business and proposed responses.
  • Executive summary
    A clear view to help you and your management team make decisions.
  • Improvement roadmap
    Practical actions in a useful order, with suggested ownership for your team.

An advisory assessment of agreed evidence. Penetration testing, vulnerability scanning and technical implementation require separate scopes and authorisation.
