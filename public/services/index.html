A clear starting point

Owl Laboratory

A guided cybersecurity conversation from Taute Group for business teams. We map what your work depends on, explore what could go wrong, and choose practical next steps together.

Best suited to

You want to understand your risk without sitting through a checkbox audit, and your team would rather argue it out in a room than fill in a spreadsheet.

What the session includes

We recap the dependencies, scenarios, safeguards, decisions and unanswered questions discussed in your session. Agreed outputs may include a draft dependency map and practical actions with owners and review dates. Interviews, evidence review, individual risk ratings, a formal risk register or a detailed roadmap can be included where useful and expressly agreed. There is no overall security score and no guarantee that every risk has been identified.

Possible agreed outputs

  • Draft dependency map of the work and systems discussed
  • Recap of scenarios, safeguards and open questions
  • Practical actions with owners and review dates

Additional interviews, evidence review, a formal risk register or a detailed roadmap are included only when expressly agreed.

Owl Laboratory is an advisory service. Taute Group does not perform penetration testing, vulnerability scanning or technical implementation. Where that work is needed, it happens under separate scopes and authorisation with specialist providers.
