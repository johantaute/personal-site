A clear starting point

Cyber Risk Discovery

We work with you to understand your risk and give you a clear starting point for improvement.

Best suited to

You want clarity on your risk, or a fresh view as your business changes.

What the assessment includes

We listen to you and your stakeholders, review available evidence, and identify your critical operations, systems, data and supplier dependencies. Together, we consider relevant threats, your existing protections and the priorities that matter to your management team.

What you receive

  • Prioritised risk register
    Your key risks, why they matter to your business and proposed responses.
  • Executive summary
    A clear view to help you and your management team make decisions.
  • Improvement roadmap
    Practical actions in a useful order, with suggested ownership for your team.

An advisory assessment of agreed evidence. Penetration testing, vulnerability scanning and technical implementation require separate scopes and authorisation.

Need a plan?

Cybersecurity Strategy & Roadmaps

A security plan shaped around your goals, your resources and the people who will carry it forward.

Best suited to
You know the risks and need a workable plan.

What you receive
  • Security objectives aligned to your business goals
  • Phased initiatives, ownership and resource considerations
  • Management decision points and a review cadence

Need ongoing guidance?

Ongoing Security Advisory

Someone to talk things through with, keep your priorities on track and support your decisions.

Best suited to
You need advice alongside your existing team or providers.

What you receive
  • Scheduled advice and action tracking
  • Guidance on supplier proposals and security decisions
  • Updated priorities as your business grows and changes

Need security leadership?

Fractional CISO

Security leadership that works alongside your team and helps you build a programme suited to your business.

Best suited to
You need security leadership without a full time appointment.

What you receive
  • Programme direction and governance guidance
  • Management risk reporting and decision support
  • Roadmap oversight with your existing teams and providers

We agree your objectives, outputs and working arrangements with you before we begin.
