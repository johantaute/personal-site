A few useful answers

Before we talk.

Is Cyber Risk Discovery a penetration test?

No. It is an advisory assessment of your business context and available evidence. Penetration testing, vulnerability scanning and technical implementation need separate scopes, authorisation and delivery arrangements.

We already have an IT provider. Can you work with them?

Yes. We work alongside internal IT and managed service providers regularly, and they are often key participants in a Discovery engagement.

Can we start with a once off engagement?

Yes. Cyber Risk Discovery can be a standalone engagement. You can decide whether ongoing guidance would be useful after we review your priorities together.

How is pricing agreed?

Pricing is agreed before work begins, based on the agreed scope. Pilot engagements are fixed-fee so there are no surprises.
