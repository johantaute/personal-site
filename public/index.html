FIELDNOTES / 02 / OWL LABORATORY

Start with the work.
Then follow the threads.

A customer request rarely belongs to one person or one system. Together, we sketch the people, information and outside services that make it possible.

Your facilitator helps the room connect them. The map becomes a shared surface for asking “what if?”, not a diagram you must prepare beforehand.

Illustrative business map. Your session starts with your context.

HOW IT WORKS

A conversation with
a way forward.

Taute Group facilitates the session; your team brings the context. Scope, participants, outputs and terms are agreed in writing before work begins.

  1. 01

    Set the context

    Choose an important piece of work and agree who should be involved.

  2. 02

    Map dependencies

    Sketch the people, information, systems and suppliers the work relies on.

  3. 03

    Explore what-ifs

    Work through realistic scenarios and discuss existing safeguards, assumptions and open questions.

  4. 04

    Choose next steps

    Agree a manageable set of actions with owners, and a recap of what was discussed.

Possible agreed outputs

A draft dependency map, a recap of scenarios and safeguards, unanswered questions, and actions with owners and review dates. Interviews, evidence review, a formal risk register or a detailed roadmap can be included where useful and expressly agreed.

Owl Laboratory is a human-facilitated discussion. No scanner, no overall security score, no audit or compliance assurance, and no guarantee that every risk is identified. Testing, scanning, implementation and monitoring require separate scopes and providers.
