FIELDNOTES / 02 / OWL LABORATORY

Start with the work.
Then follow the threads.

A customer request rarely belongs to one person or one system. Together, we sketch the people, information and outside services that make it possible.

Your facilitator helps the room connect them. The map becomes a shared surface for asking “what if?”, not a diagram you must prepare beforehand.

Illustrative map. Your session starts with your context.

HOW IT WORKS

A conversation with
a way forward.

Taute Group facilitates the session; your team brings the context. Scope, participants, outputs and terms are agreed in writing before work begins.

  1. 01

    Set the context

    Choose an important piece of work and agree who should be involved.

  2. 02

    Map dependencies

    Sketch the people, information, systems and suppliers the work relies on.

  3. 03

    Explore what-ifs

    Work through realistic scenarios and discuss existing safeguards, assumptions and open questions.

  4. 04

    Choose next steps

    Agree actions with owners and a recap of what was discussed.

START A CONVERSATION

After a session you receive a recap: the dependency map, what was discussed, and agreed actions with owners. We will discuss the focus and a suitable scope before you commit.
