How we work ↗

A few useful answers

Before we talk.

We already have an IT provider. Can you work with them?

Yes. We can work alongside your existing team and IT provider, with your agreement. We help connect technical input to your business priorities and clarify who will take each action forward.

Is Cyber Risk Discovery a penetration test?

No. It is an advisory assessment of your business context and available evidence. Penetration testing, vulnerability scanning and technical implementation need separate scopes, authorisation and delivery arrangements.

What information will you need?

We start with conversations about your operations, critical systems, data and suppliers. Useful evidence may include policies, system lists and information about existing protections. We agree what is needed and how to share it securely. Please do not send passwords or sensitive system details in an initial enquiry.

Can we start with a once off engagement?

Yes. Cyber Risk Discovery can be a standalone engagement. You can decide whether ongoing guidance would be useful after we review your priorities together.

How is pricing agreed?

We discuss your needs, agree the scope and provide a proposal with fees and deliverables before work begins. Any additional work is discussed and agreed with you separately.
