Bundi is a guided cybersecurity conversation from Taute Group for business teams. We map what your work depends on, explore what could go wrong, and choose practical next steps together.

How the conversation works

Map what your work depends on

We walk through how your business runs and what it relies on — systems, people, suppliers and information.

Explore what could go wrong

Together we explore what could go wrong for each of those dependencies — guided, practical and jargon-free.

Choose practical next steps

You leave with a short, prioritised list of next steps your team can actually take — and the reasoning behind each one.

A report that shows its work

After the session you receive a written report — the map, the risks and the agreed priorities — in language you can share with your team.

Why the name Bundi?

Bundi is a name for the owl. An owl watches quietly through the night, notices what changes and is awake before anyone else notices trouble. That is the posture we bring to your business: quiet attention, early awareness and clear words in the morning.
