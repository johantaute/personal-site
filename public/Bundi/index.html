Bundi by Taute Group

The owl that watches
while the world sleeps.

Bundi is the cybersecurity practice of Taute Group. The name comes from the owl — quiet, watchful and hard to fool. It is how we think a small business should experience cybersecurity: someone keeping watch, explaining clearly, and never making you feel foolish for asking.

What Bundi means here

Watchful, not loud

Owls see what others miss and say little. We look carefully at how your business actually works before we say anything about risk.

Built for smaller businesses

Bundi is shaped for South African small and medium businesses — not enterprise security teams with big budgets.

A guided first step

The Working Map is our guided first session. We map how your business runs, where it is exposed and what to do first — in plain language.

Advice that shows its work

Every recommendation comes with the reasoning behind it, so you can decide with confidence and explain it to your team.

Why the name Bundi?

Bundi is a name for the owl. An owl watches quietly through the night, notices what changes and is awake before anyone else notices trouble. That is the posture we bring to your business: quiet attention, early awareness and clear words in the morning.
